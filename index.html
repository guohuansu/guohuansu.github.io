Research interests

I combine species distributions, functional traits and river networks to study how freshwater diversity is assembled and how it responds to disturbance.

Fish biodiversity

How geography and environmental history shape fish diversity across river networks.

Biological invasions

How species traits and recipient communities influence the establishment of non-native fishes.

Functional ecology

How morphology and life-history traits reveal differences among freshwater communities.

Global change

How dams, climate change and other human pressures alter fish communities and conservation priorities.
