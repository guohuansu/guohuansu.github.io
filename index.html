Research

Questions that flow across scales

From individual morphology to global river basins, I study the processes shaping freshwater biodiversity and the consequences of environmental change.

01

Fish biodiversity

How river networks, geography and environmental history shape taxonomic, functional and phylogenetic diversity.

02

Biological invasions

Why non-native fishes establish, how communities resist invasion and which traits drive ecological success.

03

Functional ecology

Using morphology and life-history traits to reveal assembly processes and ecosystem responses to disturbance.

04

Global change

Measuring the effects of dams, climate change and human pressure on freshwater communities and conservation priorities.
