Showed marked changes in freshwater fish biodiversity

Human impacts on freshwater fish worldwide

How much have human activities changed freshwater fish diversity worldwide?

We compared fish assemblages across river basins using a measure that combines changes in species, functional traits and evolutionary history. The study revealed widespread biodiversity change and identified river basins with relatively low impacts.

Human impacts on global freshwater fish biodiversity

Nature Communications · 2023

North American freshwater fishes

What makes a fish invasion succeed?

Why do some introduced fishes establish, and why are some communities more vulnerable to invasion?

We analysed traits and community composition across more than 1,800 North American fish communities. Reproductive traits and human use helped explain which species established, while the functional structure of recipient communities helped explain their vulnerability.

Species invasiveness and community invasibility of North American freshwater fish fauna revealed via trait-based analysis

Historical and recent Yangtze fish occurrences

Tracing fish biodiversity change in the Yangtze

Where has fish biodiversity changed most in the Yangtze River Basin?

We compared historical and recent fish occurrences across 56 geographic units. More than 80% showed declines in species, functional and phylogenetic richness. The middle reaches showed the greatest overall reorganization under the CCBF index.

Human impacts on fish biodiversity in the Yangtze River Basin: a multidimensional assessment using the Cumulative Change in Biodiversity Facets (CCBF) index

A DNA reference library for Yangtze fishes

How can environmental DNA surveys identify Yangtze fishes more consistently?

We curated a regional fish species list and assembled reference sequences from multiple databases. The resource supports taxonomic assignment in environmental DNA surveys and helps identify gaps in reference coverage.

YZFishDB: A curated DNA reference database for the fishes of the Yangtze River Basin

Measuring aquaculture’s benefits and costs

How do the social benefits and environmental costs of aquaculture compare across countries?

We evaluated aquaculture across 161 countries using social and environmental indicators. The assessment identifies where changes in production practices could improve sustainability.
